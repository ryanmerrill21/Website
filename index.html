About

An engineer who likes the people side of the problem.

I grew up in Brevard, a small mountain town in Western North Carolina, and I'm now a third-year mechanical engineering student at Duke's Pratt School of Engineering. Engineering taught me to take systems apart. Working with people taught me that most systems only improve when the people inside them buy in.

That's the thread through my work: a safety dashboard on a data center job site, a product feature pitched to Ancestry's product team, and a 150-member student organization I lead for rural students like me. I'm looking for roles in product and analytics where I can keep doing both halves.

Safety KPI Dashboard

Problem
Field safety data at a data center build lived in scattered reports, so leadership couldn't see when or where incidents clustered.
What I did
Built an Excel dashboard joining field hours, incident types, safety reports and day-of-week trends to track Total Case Incident Rate and trade-partner performance.
Result
